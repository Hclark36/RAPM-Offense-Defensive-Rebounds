What goes into the model

Player effects
Separate offensive and defensive lineup indicators for every player. Unseen players in the final test receive zero raw player effects.
Missed-shot context
Missed two versus three, whether the shot was blocked, and whether the offense was at home. The intercept and context controls are unpenalized.
Historical player reports
The ratings in the explorer come from a separate refit using all three seasons, after the frozen-model evaluation. Role ratings are centered on the exposure-weighted average player.
Data scope
Public NBA play-by-play, with a pinned SportsDataverse schedule and player catalogue. Eligible team rebounds are included. Missed free throws, placeholder rebounds, invalid lineups, and unresolved recoveries are excluded and audited.
Research limitsThese estimates describe association with team recovery. Teammates who share most of their minutes are difficult to separate, and scheme, positioning, transition responsibilities, and lineup assignment are incompletely measured. Player-level uncertainty and alternate-window sensitivity analyses remain future work. An on-floor missed-shot opportunity differs from a tracking-defined individual rebound chance.
